✨ AI agent

Claude, Anthropic's AI, looks at each photo the way you would and fixes the cut-out: it keeps the plate under the food and the hands holding the subject, removes leftovers of the background, and checks its own work. It selects objects with a segmentation model that runs in your browser.

Create one at console.anthropic.com. This page sends it only to Anthropic's API.

With a key, a 1024-pixel copy of each photo the agent checks is sent to Anthropic, and the use of Claude Opus 5.5 is billed to your API account: a few cents per photo (the estimate is shown under each photo). Without a key everything stays on your device, and you can still keep or remove objects by hand with "Edit selection".
